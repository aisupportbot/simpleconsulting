How to Manage Your Amazon Account Health Dashboard

By Scott Nguyen · Published Oct 13, 2025 · Updated 2026-09-15

In this article
  1. Read the specific metric and notice
  2. Investigate the underlying records
  3. Use the current requirements
  4. Assign ownership and document the response

Amazon account health should be monitored using the dashboard, notices and policies applicable to your account and fulfilment model.

Read the specific metric and notice

Check which orders, dates and issue types contribute to an alert. Order defects, shipment performance, cancellations and policy compliance are different problems and may require different responses.

Investigate the underlying records

Match the issue with the relevant order, shipment or customer-service record. Confirm whether the root cause is stock accuracy, dispatch, product information, documentation or another process.

Use the current requirements

Thresholds, programme requirements and response routes can change. Use the policies and deadlines shown for your marketplace instead of relying on a historical list of numbers.

Assign ownership and document the response

Give someone responsibility for reviewing the account at a cadence suitable to the risk and workload. Track outstanding actions and follow the requested process. An appeal or corrective action does not guarantee reinstatement.
