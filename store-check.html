  1. High impact · Trust

  2. High impact · Product pages

    Homepage images total 9.4 MB and take about 6 seconds to appear on mobile.

    Fix: Compress and resize hero images; lazy-load images below the fold.

  4. Medium · First impression

    The homepage headline is a slogan and the first button says “Explore”.

    Fix: Say what you sell and who it is for; make the first button go to best sellers.

  5. Low · Search basics

    Product pages share one generic meta description.

    Fix: Write a short unique description for your top 20 products.

What’s checked

Six areas that decide whether a visitor buys.

Speed & mobile

How fast the page loads on a phone, layout shifts, and oversized images that slow shoppers down.

First impression

Whether a visitor can tell what you sell, why it is better and what to click within a few seconds.

Product pages

Titles, images, descriptions, variants and price clarity: the details that decide add-to-cart.

Trust

Reviews, shipping costs, return policy and contact details, and whether shoppers can find them before checkout.

Search basics

Page titles, descriptions, headings and product data that Google and AI search read.

Marketplace listings

Amazon, Walmart and Best Buy titles, bullets, description, images and reviews, checked against each marketplace’s rules, with a rewritten title and bullets.

Questions

Before you run it.

Is the store check really free?

Yes. It costs nothing and there is no obligation. If the report shows issues worth fixing, you can fix them yourself or ask about a rebuild.

Do you need access to my store?

No. The check reads the same public pages your customers see. No logins, passwords or app installs.

Will I be added to a mailing list?

No. You get your report, and Scott may follow up once about the findings. Every email includes a way to opt out.

Does it check Amazon, Walmart or Best Buy listings?

Yes. Choose “My marketplace listing” and paste the title, bullets and description. Marketplaces block automated tools from reading listings, so pasting is the reliable way. A full account review, with advertising and inventory, is part of the channel diagnostic.
