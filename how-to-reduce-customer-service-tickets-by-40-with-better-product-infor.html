How to Reduce Customer Service Tickets With Better Product Information

By Scott Nguyen · Published Jun 16, 2025 · Updated 2026-09-15 · 1 min read

In this article
  1. Audit Your Ticket Themes
  2. Fix the Listing First
  3. Build a FAQ Section
  4. Use Post-Purchase Emails Proactively

Repeated customer questions can reveal missing or unclear product information. Establish the actual pattern from your own tickets before setting a reduction target.

Audit Your Ticket Themes

Export a suitable sample of recent tickets and group them by question. Use the actual counts to identify which topics deserve attention first.

Fix the Listing First

If customers repeatedly ask about size, compatibility, or usage, that information needs to be prominent in your listing — not buried in the description.

Build a FAQ Section

Build your FAQ directly from ticket data — questions customers actually ask, not ones you assume they have.

Use Post-Purchase Emails Proactively

A post-purchase email answering the most common questions before customers need to ask dramatically reduces ticket volume.

The fix: Pull your top 10 ticket categories. For each, identify whether a listing update, FAQ, or post-purchase email would have prevented it. Implement fixes for your top two this month.
