Walmart management

Walmart Marketplace & WFS. Managed from setup to daily operations.

Launching on Walmart is one step. Keeping the catalogue, advertising and inventory working together is the ongoing job. I help brands build and manage their Walmart channel, with particular attention to selling in Canada.

Walmart seller setup and catalogue management

Prepare the product information and operating setup your chosen marketplace requires. I help coordinate the application and onboarding process, item setup and catalogue maintenance. Walmart determines account and product approval.

  • Category and product-attribute requirements
  • Item feeds, identifiers and variant setup
  • Listing content, images and publishing issues
  • Country-specific requirements and French-content coordination where needed

Walmart Fulfillment Services support

Choose a fulfilment approach using your product dimensions, landed costs, expected volume and return handling. I help plan inbound shipments and replenishment, then review receiving and inventory records.

  • WFS versus self-fulfilment cost comparisons
  • Inbound shipping, labelling and receiving coordination
  • Stock cover, reorder timing and seasonal planning
  • Returns and settlement reconciliation, including prior credits

Walmart Connect advertising

Review the campaign formats and reports available in your market. I connect campaign decisions with item availability, pricing and margin so advertising is part of the channel operating plan.

  • Campaign structure, product selection and budget review
  • Search-term and performance analysis where reports are available
  • Listing readiness before increasing spend
  • Reporting with clear attribution periods

A practical launch decision

Before a launch, compare the product margin after marketplace fees, fulfilment, inbound freight, returns and advertising assumptions. If you are already selling, start with the catalogue, stock or reporting issue that needs attention. Canadian and US programme requirements are checked separately.

Questions before we start

Is Walmart a good next channel after Amazon?

That depends on your category, price positioning, margins and ability to fulfil orders. We review those factors before recommending a launch.

Can you help with unpublished items?

Yes. I review the item status, feed errors and account notices to identify what is blocking publication. Resolution depends on the issue and Walmart's requirements.

Are Canadian and US WFS rules the same?

Do not assume they are. Available services, fees, reports and operating requirements should be checked for the country and programme you use.

Do you review WFS reimbursements?

Yes. I reconcile shipment, return and settlement records and assess potential claim eligibility. Reimbursement is subject to the applicable rules, evidence, deadlines and Walmart's approval.
