Google Ads

How to Use Performance Max Campaigns Without Losing Control

By Scott Nguyen · Published Jan 19, 2026 · 1 min read

In this article
  1. Provide Strong Asset Groups
  2. Use Audience Signals
  3. Set Brand Exclusions

Performance Max campaigns serve ads across Search, Shopping, Display, YouTube, Gmail, and Maps from a single campaign. They can deliver exceptional results — or burn budget if configured incorrectly.

Provide Strong Asset Groups

PMax performance is directly tied to the quality of assets you provide — headlines, descriptions, images, and videos. Campaigns with minimal assets give Google limited options. Invest in your creative before launching.

Use Audience Signals

Audience Signals tell Google who to target first as the campaign learns. Provide Customer Match lists, website visitors, and In-Market audiences as signals. Better signals mean faster, more efficient optimization.

Set Brand Exclusions

PMax will serve ads on brand keyword searches by default — attributing those conversions to PMax rather than your more efficient branded Search campaign. Apply brand exclusions to prevent this overlap.

The fix: If you're running PMax, check your Audience Signals. If you're not providing Customer Match or website visitor audiences, add them immediately — this is the highest-leverage improvement for most underperforming PMax campaigns.
