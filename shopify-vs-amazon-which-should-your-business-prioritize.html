Shopify vs Amazon: Which Should Your Business Prioritize?

By Scott Nguyen · Published Jan 2, 2025 · Updated 2026-09-15

In this article
  1. Compare the full cost of a sale
  2. Assess how customers will find you
  3. Respect the customer relationship and platform rules
  4. Choose a workable first phase

Amazon and Shopify serve different commercial roles. The right priority depends on demand, product margins, customer acquisition and the operating capacity behind each channel.

Compare the full cost of a sale

For Amazon, include the applicable referral and fulfilment fees, storage, returns and advertising. For Shopify, include payment processing, store costs, fulfilment, returns and acquisition. Do not assume direct sales always produce more profit.

Assess how customers will find you

A marketplace provides a shopping environment with existing visitors; your own store requires a plan to attract relevant traffic. Estimate demand using the evidence available for your products rather than borrowing a platform-wide market statistic.

Respect the customer relationship and platform rules

Use each channel’s permitted tools for service and marketing. Do not build a strategy around diverting marketplace transactions or collecting marketplace buyer details for unrelated promotion. Build direct marketing through appropriately consented relationships.

Choose a workable first phase

Model a small assortment on both channels, including stock and cash needs. Give each task an owner. Expand when the economics and operating evidence support the decision.
