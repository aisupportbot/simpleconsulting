Questions before we start

Do you work directly in Seller Central?

Where account access is appropriate, use Amazon permission controls rather than sharing a password. We agree on the scope and access needed before work begins.

Can you help with FBA issues?

Yes. The work can include inbound planning, receiving discrepancies, inventory questions and the records needed to understand an issue. Amazon determines account and reimbursement decisions.

Can you work with an existing agency or employee?

Yes. I can own a defined set of Seller Central tasks and coordinate with your existing team, agency or operations provider.
