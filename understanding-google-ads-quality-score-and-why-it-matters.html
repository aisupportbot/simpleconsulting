Understanding Google Ads Quality Score and Why It Matters

By Scott Nguyen · Published Sep 25, 2024 · Updated 2026-09-15

In this article
  1. Use the components to find questions
  2. Check relevance from search to landing page
  3. Judge changes using business results

Quality Score is a Google Ads diagnostic for Search keywords. It can help identify relevance and experience issues, but the reported 1–10 score is not itself an input to the ad auction.

Use the components to find questions

Review expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing-page experience. A below-average component suggests where to investigate; it does not prescribe one fix or predict a specific cost saving.

Check relevance from search to landing page

Does the search match the service? Does the ad explain that service accurately? Does the destination answer the question and provide an appropriate next step?

Judge changes using business results

Compare qualified enquiries or sales with spend, while considering auction conditions and attribution. Raising a diagnostic score is not the same as improving profit. Read Google’s explanation of Quality Score.
