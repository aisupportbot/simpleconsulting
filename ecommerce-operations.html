Inventory & fulfilment

Get ahead of inventory and fulfilment problems.

Stockouts, excess inventory, unclear warehouse charges and returns backlogs compete for your time and cash. I help you understand what is happening and put practical buying, fulfilment and follow-up processes in place.

Inventory forecasting and replenishment

Turn sales history, supplier lead times and stock positions into an actionable buying plan. We account for seasonality, upcoming promotions, minimum order quantities and cash constraints rather than relying on one rule for every SKU.

  • SKU-level sales and stock-cover review
  • Reorder points, safety-stock assumptions and open purchase orders
  • Seasonal and launch inventory planning
  • Slow-moving stock and replenishment exceptions

3PL evaluation and fulfilment costs

Compare warehouses using your actual order mix and service requirements. I help organise rate comparisons, receiving and returns requirements, integration questions and operating responsibilities.

  • Receiving, storage, pick-and-pack and returns cost comparisons
  • Inbound routing, labelling and delivery coordination
  • Inventory discrepancy and shrinkage processes
  • Service expectations, reporting and exit planning

Returns and repeatable processes

Build a clear route from return receipt to inspection, reconciliation and an appropriate disposition. Product safety, condition and channel rules come before any resale decision. Document the recurring tasks so the team can repeat them reliably.

  • Returns status and responsibility tracking
  • Receiving and shipment reconciliation
  • Standard operating procedures and handover
  • A weekly review of exceptions and outstanding actions

Start where the cost is visible

Bring one recurring problem: a SKU that keeps stocking out, an unclear 3PL invoice or a returns backlog. We can begin with a focused review and decide whether a project or ongoing operating support is the right fit.

A good fit

Inventory or fulfilment problems keep interrupting your team across marketplaces, Shopify or retail.

What you can expect

The relevant buying plan, cost comparison, exception tracker or operating procedure, with clear ownership and a usable handover.

Questions before we start

Can you help if I do not sell on Amazon or Walmart?

Yes. Inventory, fulfilment and process work can support Shopify, wholesale and other retail channels. We confirm the systems and responsibilities before agreeing on a scope.

Do you operate a warehouse?

No. I help evaluate, coordinate and improve fulfilment operations with your team and providers. Warehouse services and freight are supplied by the relevant providers.

Can you review our existing 3PL?

Yes. A review can compare invoices, agreed rates, service performance, inventory accuracy and the handling of receiving or returns issues.

What should I bring to the first call?

Describe the problem and your channels. Relevant sales, inventory or fulfilment records can be agreed afterwards; no account access is needed to start.
