3PL & fulfilment consultant · Canada

Choose and operate a 3PL around the work your channels actually require.

30 minutes · Work directly with Scott · No preparation needed

Define the requirements before comparing providers

Document order volume, SKU profile, storage needs, receiving patterns, parcel mix, marketplace requirements, returns and systems before asking 3PLs to quote. A low pick fee is not useful if important operating work is missing.

Compare the complete cost structure

Review receiving, storage, pick-and-pack, packaging, parcel, return, special-project, account-management and exit costs using the same operating assumptions across providers.

Set measurable operating expectations

Clarify service levels, reporting, inventory accuracy, order cutoffs, escalation routes, claims, shrinkage handling and what evidence the 3PL returns when something goes wrong.

Manage the handoffs after launch

A 3PL transition is not complete when inventory arrives. I can help coordinate marketplace feeds, stock reconciliation, inbound receipts, returns, exceptions and the recurring operating review.

Questions before we start

Can you help choose a 3PL?

Yes. I can help define requirements, compare proposals and identify operating questions or cost gaps before a decision.

Do you receive referral fees from 3PLs?

Simple Consulting is positioned as independent consulting. Any commercial relationship relevant to a recommendation should be disclosed before it influences an engagement.

Can you help after the warehouse is selected?

Yes. Onboarding, data handoffs, inventory reconciliation, SLA tracking and exception management are often as important as the selection itself.

Do you work with marketplace fulfilment as well as 3PLs?

Yes. The operating model can include FBA, WFS, retailer fulfilment requirements and third-party warehouses where those channels are in scope.

What keeps landing back on your desk?

Tell me about the channel or recurring issue. We will talk through where help would make a difference and a practical next step.
