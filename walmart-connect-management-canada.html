Walmart Connect management · Canada

Walmart advertising managed alongside the catalogue and inventory behind it.

I help brands review and manage Walmart advertising with the product, listing and stock position in view. The aim is to make campaign decisions that your wider Walmart operation can support.

Check product readiness before increasing spend

Advertising cannot solve an unavailable item or an unclear listing. I review the products being promoted, their publishing status, price, inventory and the customer-facing offer before treating campaign settings as the only issue.

  • Campaign and product selection
  • Budget and bidding settings available in the account
  • Search-term and performance reports where available
  • Listing and inventory dependencies

Use the available reports to make documented decisions

I review performance using the reports and attribution periods available in the relevant Walmart account. Changes are recorded with the reason, owner and next review point instead of relying on a one-time dashboard snapshot.

Keep advertising connected to margin and stock

A campaign can create demand faster than inventory can be replenished. I connect advertising choices with product economics, sellable stock, inbound timing and the priorities of the broader marketplace account.

Manage Walmart Connect as part of the channel

Walmart Connect can be a focused workstream or part of ongoing Walmart Marketplace management. Available campaign formats, reporting and programme features should be confirmed for the country and account you use.

Questions before we start

Do you manage Walmart Sponsored Products?

I can manage the advertising formats available in the relevant Walmart Connect account. Formats and reporting vary by market and can change, so the live account is the source of truth.

Can you review campaigns without taking over the account?

Yes. A scoped review can focus on campaign structure, product selection, search terms, budgets, measurement and listing or inventory constraints.

Do you guarantee a target ROAS?

No. Results depend on demand, price, competition, product content, inventory and measurement. We agree on the business measures to review and document the changes made.

Can you manage Walmart listings and ads together?

Yes. Listing optimization and advertising can be coordinated when both are in scope.

What keeps landing back on your desk?

Tell me about the channel or recurring issue. We will talk through where help would make a difference and a practical next step.
