How to Build Backlinks for a Service Business Without a PR Budget

By Scott Nguyen · Jun 23, 2025 · 1 min read

Backlinks from other websites are one of the most important factors in Google's ranking algorithm. Most small service businesses assume building them requires a PR agency. It doesn't.

Guest Posts on Industry Publications

Write a genuinely useful article for a publication your target clients read. Most industry blogs accept guest contributions from practitioners with real expertise. Pitch with a specific article idea.

Get Listed in Relevant Directories

Industry directories, local business directories, and professional association listings are straightforward backlink sources. Google Business Profile, your local Chamber of Commerce, and industry-specific directories all provide legitimate backlinks.

Create Linkable Assets

A genuinely useful guide, checklist, or template attracts backlinks naturally because other sites want to reference it. The best linkable assets solve a specific problem comprehensively.

Partner With Complementary Businesses

Businesses serving the same audience with different services are natural backlink partners — links on each other's sites create mutual value and legitimate backlinks for both.

The fix: Identify three industry publications your ideal clients read. Pitch one guest article idea to each. A 30% acceptance rate means three pitches should produce at least one placement.
