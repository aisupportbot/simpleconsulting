How to Choose the Right ERP for a Growing E-commerce Business

By Scott Nguyen · Sep 4, 2024 · 1 min read

Choosing an ERP is one of the highest-stakes technology decisions a growing e-commerce business makes. The implementation process is expensive, the switching cost is enormous, and the wrong choice can consume management bandwidth for years. Here's how to approach the decision systematically.

Define Your Requirements Before Talking to Vendors

Every ERP vendor will tell you their system can do everything you need. Before you start vendor conversations, document your specific requirements: What integrations do you need? What reports do you need to run? What processes are currently manual that need to be automated? What's your budget, including implementation — not just license fees? Walk into every vendor conversation with your requirements document.

Total Cost of Ownership, Not License Cost

The license fee is typically the smallest component of ERP cost. Implementation, customization, data migration, training, and ongoing support often cost 3–5x the annual license fee. Get a comprehensive cost estimate that includes all of these before making a decision.

Evaluate Based on Your Industry's Requirements

Generic ERPs require significant customization to fit industry-specific needs. Look for systems with e-commerce-specific functionality: native integrations with your marketplaces and sales channels, inventory management built for multi-location and multi-channel, and order management that handles marketplace-specific requirements.

Reference Checks Are Non-Negotiable

Ask every vendor for three references from businesses similar to yours in size and industry. Call all three. Ask about implementation timeline vs promise, hidden costs, and whether they would choose the same system again.

The fix: Before contacting a single ERP vendor, spend two hours documenting your top 20 system requirements ranked by importance. This single document will save you months of wasted vendor conversations.
