How to Expand From Amazon to Walmart Without Killing Your Margins

By Scott Nguyen · May 14, 2025 · 1 min read

Walmart Marketplace has grown significantly and represents a real opportunity for brands already on Amazon. But the expansion traps sellers who assume it works the same way.

Walmart Is Not Amazon

The buyer intent, search algorithm, content requirements, and fulfillment expectations are all different. Copy-pasting Amazon listings to Walmart typically produces poor results.

The Pricing Trap

Walmart's algorithm is heavily price-sensitive. If your product is priced higher on Walmart than elsewhere, Walmart will suppress your listing. Channel-specific pricing strategy is the answer.

WFS vs Self-Fulfillment

Walmart Fulfillment Services improves search visibility and unlocks the Walmart+ badge. But model the fees specifically for your product before committing.

The fix: Model your WFS unit economics before listing a single product. Build Walmart-specific content from scratch rather than repurposing Amazon assets.
