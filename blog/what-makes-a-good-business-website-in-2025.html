What Makes a Good Business Website in 2025

By Scott Nguyen · Dec 4, 2024 · 1 min read

The standard for what looks professional online has risen dramatically. A website that would have seemed impressive in 2018 now looks dated and untrustworthy. Here's what a good business website needs in 2025.

It Loads in Under 2 Seconds on Mobile

Page speed is both a user experience and a ranking factor. 53% of mobile users abandon sites that take more than 3 seconds to load. Your site needs to load fast — not just on your office WiFi, but on an average mobile connection.

The Value Proposition Is Clear in 5 Seconds

A visitor to your homepage should understand what you do, who you do it for, and why they should choose you — without scrolling. If they have to hunt for this information, you're losing them.

Social Proof Is Prominent

Testimonials, case studies, client logos, and reviews should appear above the fold or within the first scroll. Buyers need to see that others have trusted you before they will.

Every Page Has a Clear Next Step

Every page on your site should guide the visitor toward one specific action. Book a call. Get a quote. Download a resource. View a product. Don't make them decide what to do — tell them.

It's Accessible and ADA Compliant

Accessibility isn't just the right thing to do — it affects SEO and protects you from legal risk. Proper heading structure, image alt text, sufficient colour contrast, and keyboard navigation are the basics.

The fix: Test your site on your phone on a cellular connection. If you'd be embarrassed to show it to a potential client, it's time for a redesign.
