How to Send Inventory to Walmart WFS in Canada: Prep, Labels and Routing

Short answer: Create an inbound order in Seller Center first, label every unit with a scannable 12-digit UPC, pack to Walmart's carton and pallet rules, and ship only to the fulfilment centre named on your order. Parcel deliveries of up to 10 boxes don't need an appointment. LTL and full-truckload deliveries do. Most delayed or refused WFS shipments come from preventable problems: the wrong barcode, a missing expiry date, poor packaging or a missed appointment.

Walmart's WFS routing guide is the source of truth and is updated several times a year. This guide summarises the practical points from the Canadian guides available in November 2026, so check the current version in Seller Center before each new type of shipment.

1. Create the inbound order before anything ships

Go to WFS → Shipments → Send Inventory in Seller Center. You can create an order for one SKU at a time or upload Walmart's template for several SKUs. You'll enter a ship-from and return address, an expected delivery date, the units per SKU and how they are packed into master cases.

  • First shipment of a SKU: fewer than 150 units.
  • Any shipment: fewer than 500 SKUs.
  • Prep services: choose Walmart's labelling service on the order if you want it. You can't change prep settings after submitting; you'd have to cancel and recreate the order.

Inventory that arrives without an inbound order can be delayed or rejected at check-in.

2. Label every unit correctly

Each sellable unit needs one scannable 12-digit UPC on its outermost packaging. You can download item labels from Seller Center.

  • Not accepted: 14-digit GTIN or vendor SKU barcodes, Amazon FNSKU labels, labels with leading zeros (00) and labels printed for Walmart US.
  • Variants: every size, colour or pack count needs its own UPC.
  • Remove: price tags and other retailers' labels.

If you'd rather not label units yourself, Walmart's prep service applies UPC labels for $0.45 per unit when you request it on the inbound order. Units that arrive needing unplanned label fixes are charged $0.65 per unit, and Walmart says that work can take up to 10 business days.

3. Pack cartons to Walmart's standard

WFS Canada carton requirements
RequirementWhat to do
Box typeSturdy regular slotted cartons (ECT-32 or 200 lb burst strength). No gaylords or display shippers.
WeightNo more than 50 lb per box unless a single unit is heavier. Add Team Lift labels over 50 lb and mechanical lift labels over 100 lb.
LabelsThe WFS shipment label on every box, with boxes numbered "Carton X of Y". Remove old labels from reused boxes.
Mixed SKUsSeparate different SKUs inside a box with a partition.
DunnageAir pillows, bubble wrap, foam or full sheets of paper. No packing peanuts, crinkle wrap or shredded paper.
ClosureFlaps closed with gaps of 1 inch or less. No open-top cases, and don't tape or strap boxes together.

4. Polybags, sets, fragile items and expiry dates

  • Polybags: clear and sealed, with the barcode scannable through or on the bag. Bags with an opening of 5 inches or more need a suffocation warning. Opaque bags get re-bagged at the fulfilment centre, which delays receiving.
  • Sets and multi-piece items: package everything for one sellable unit together and mark sets "Sold as set". The fulfilment centre will not assemble items.
  • Glass and ceramics: wrap each unit and pack it in a six-sided box that can survive repeated drops, and mark the outer box as fragile.
  • Liquids: use leak-proof packaging in a sealed carton with "This side up" arrows.
  • Expiry-dated products: need at least 60 days of shelf life when received, and Walmart removes them from sale 30 days before expiry. Print the date on the outer packaging, preferably as YYYY-MM-DD, with the month in English and French or bilingual symbols. Lot codes and production dates don't count, and a missing date is treated as damage.

WFS Canada does not accept perishable or regulated products, and aerosols are not allowed. Dry grocery and natural health products need Walmart's approval before you send them.

5. Build pallets for LTL shipments

  • Use a 40 × 48 inch, four-way wooden pallet in good condition. No cardboard pallets.
  • Keep each pallet under 1,500 lb and 72 inches tall, including the pallet, with no overhang.
  • Put a shipment ID label on all four sides, on the outside of the stretch wrap.
  • Keep the same SKU together, and for date-coded products, put the earliest dates on top.

6. Ship to the right fulfilment centre

Ship only to the fulfilment centre on your inbound order. Misrouted shipments are re-routed at your expense. Walmart's August 2026 routing guide lists Mississauga for sortable items, Milton for non-sortable items, and Calgary for both.

A unit is sortable when it weighs under 33 lb, its longest side is under 30 inches and its other two sides are under 18 inches. Anything heavier or larger is non-sortable. The maximum accepted unit is 150 lb.

7. Choose parcel, Walmart Preferred Carrier or LTL

WFS Canada inbound shipping options
MethodBest forKey rules
Your own parcel carrierSmall shipmentsNo appointment. Up to 10 boxes per delivery, and only items that can travel on a conveyor.
Walmart Preferred Carrier (FedEx parcel)Small shipments from anywhere in Canada at Walmart's negotiated ratesBoxes up to 70 lb, 48 inches on the longest side, 30 inches on the second side and 105 inches in length plus girth. You have 24 hours to void the estimate. Not available for dangerous goods, food, drugs, OTC or natural health products. More than 10 packages needs a delivery appointment.
LTL or full truckloadPalletised or larger shipmentsBook the delivery appointment in Seller Center before adding tracking. One PO per shipment and one appointment per trailer.

For LTL, the bill of lading must list every shipment ID, the full fulfilment centre address and an accurate carton count. "Said to contain" bills of lading aren't accepted. Trucks need to arrive close to their appointment time, and cancellations should be emailed to the fulfilment centre at least 24 hours ahead.

Walmart's Preferred Carrier fee is discounted 100% until January 31, 2027. For a valid claim on a Preferred Carrier shipment lost or damaged in transit, Walmart's terms reimburse shipping fees and up to 50% of the goods' value, to a maximum of $4,000 per FedEx parcel shipment. The WFS reimbursements guide covers how those claims work.

8. If a 3PL or supplier ships for you

The fulfilment centre doesn't care who packs the shipment, but your account carries the consequences if it's wrong. Agree who owns each step before the first shipment:

WFS shipment handoff with a 3PL or supplier
TaskUsually owned by
Create the inbound order and print labelsYou (in Seller Center)
Apply unit, carton and pallet labels3PL or supplier
Book the delivery appointment (LTL)You or the 3PL, agreed in writing
Send tracking, BOL and packing list3PL or carrier, returned to you
Photos of labelled cartons and pallets before pickup3PL or supplier

Give the 3PL a one-page WFS spec covering labels, carton weight, dunnage, pallet height and label placement. Ask for photos of finished cartons and pallets before pickup. Those photos are also your best evidence if Walmart later reports damage or a shortage. The 3PL and fulfilment consulting page covers how to set up those handoffs.

9. Pre-ship checklist

  • Inbound order created, with quantities that match what's being packed
  • Every unit has a scannable 12-digit UPC, with no FNSKU or other retailer labels
  • Expiry dates visible, in the right format, with at least 60 days remaining
  • Cartons under 50 lb, labelled "Carton X of Y" with the WFS shipment label
  • Pallets under 72 inches and 1,500 lb, labelled on all four sides
  • Shipping to the fulfilment centre on the order
  • Appointment booked for LTL, or no more than 10 boxes for parcel
  • BOL, packing list and photos saved with the shipment ID

10. After delivery

Watch the order's status on the Shipments page as it moves from In Transit to Receiving in Progress to Closed. If Walmart flags an exception, such as a shortage, damage or incorrect prep, respond quickly. Walmart's guides say that if it gets no response within 7 business days, it may close the case or return the inventory according to your return preferences.

If units were delivered but not fully received, use the WFS delivered but not received guide and the reimbursement checklist. To check costs before you ship, read Walmart WFS fees in Canada. If you'd like someone to set up the shipments and the 3PL handoff, see Walmart WFS consulting.

Frequently asked questions

What labels does Walmart WFS Canada require?

Every sellable unit needs a scannable 12-digit UPC on its outer packaging, every carton needs the WFS shipment label, and every pallet needs a shipment ID label on all four sides. Amazon FNSKU labels, 14-digit GTIN barcodes and labels with leading zeros are not accepted.

Do I need an appointment to ship to WFS in Canada?

Not for parcel deliveries of up to 10 boxes. LTL and full-truckload deliveries need an appointment booked in Seller Center, and Walmart Preferred Carrier shipments of more than 10 packages also need one.

Can Walmart label my products for me?

Yes. Select the labelling prep service when you create the inbound order. Walmart's Canadian prep guide lists $0.45 per unit for requested labelling and $0.65 per unit for unplanned label fixes at the fulfilment centre.

How much shelf life do expiry-dated products need for WFS?

At least 60 days when the fulfilment centre receives them. Walmart removes expiry-dated items from sale 30 days before the date, so plan quantities you can sell within that window.

Can my 3PL or supplier ship directly to WFS?

Generally yes. You still create the inbound order in your Seller Center and share the labels, and the shipment must meet the same labelling, packing and appointment rules. Agree in writing who applies labels, books appointments and keeps the shipping records.
