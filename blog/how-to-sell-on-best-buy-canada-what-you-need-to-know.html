How to Sell on Best Buy Canada: What You Need to Know

By Scott Nguyen · Oct 23, 2024 · 1 min read

Best Buy Canada is one of the most important retail channels for consumer electronics, appliances, and home technology products in Canada. Getting listed is a significant opportunity — but the channel has specific requirements and economics that need to be understood before you pursue it.

How Best Buy Canada Buying Works

Best Buy Canada operates primarily as a traditional buyer-vendor relationship. A category buyer manages each product area and makes assortment decisions. Getting a meeting requires a warm introduction, a strong sales story, and product data that meets their submission standards — category velocity, margin contribution, and competitive positioning.

The Economics to Understand

Best Buy Canada typically works on a cost-plus model where they negotiate a cost price from you and set their own retail price. They will expect a competitive cost that allows them to maintain their standard category margin. Marketing development funds (MDF), co-op advertising, and return reserves are all common expectations.

Content and Data Requirements

Best Buy Canada requires product content in their specific format — images, specifications, and product copy that meets their standards. Getting this wrong delays your launch and creates friction with the buyer relationship. Have your product content completely ready before your first buyer meeting.

Logistics and Compliance

Best Buy Canada typically requires EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) compliance, specific labelling standards, and routing guide compliance for shipments. Non-compliance results in chargebacks that can erode your margin significantly.

The fix: If you're pursuing Best Buy Canada, start by researching which category buyer manages your product type. Prepare a one-page sell-in sheet with your product's velocity data, margin story, and competitive positioning before you make contact.
