How to Set Up Google Ads for Lead Generation: Step-by-Step

By Scott Nguyen · Apr 13, 2026 · 1 min read

Setting up Google Ads for lead generation requires a different approach than shopping or awareness campaigns. Here's a step-by-step setup that produces qualified leads efficiently.

Step 1: Define Your Conversion Actions

Before building any campaign, define what counts as a lead. A form submission? A phone call? A booking? Set up conversion tracking for each action. For phone calls, use Google's call conversion tracking with a duration threshold — a call under 30 seconds is rarely a genuine lead.

Step 2: Build Service-Specific Landing Pages

Each campaign should direct traffic to a landing page specific to the service being advertised — not your homepage. Remove the navigation menu from landing pages to prevent visitors from wandering away.

Step 3: Start With Exact and Phrase Match Keywords

Build your initial keyword list around 10–20 highly specific, high-intent terms that indicate the searcher is actively looking to hire rather than just researching the topic.

Step 4: Review and Iterate Weekly

Check your search term report weekly. Add irrelevant terms to your negative keyword list. Pause keywords generating clicks without conversions. The discipline of weekly review separates profitable lead generation from budget burning.

The fix: If you're running lead generation on Google Ads without call conversion tracking, implement it today. Phone calls are often the highest-converting lead type for service businesses and missing them means flying blind on your most valuable conversions.
