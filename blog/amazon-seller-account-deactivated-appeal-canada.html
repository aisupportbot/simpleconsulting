Amazon Seller Account Deactivated? How to Appeal in Canada

An Amazon seller account deactivation can interrupt sales, inventory plans and cash flow. If your Amazon.ca selling account has been deactivated, the first step is not to copy an appeal template. It is to identify the exact reason in Seller Central and follow the instructions for that specific case.

This guide focuses on responding after an account deactivation. For ongoing monitoring and prevention, see our separate Amazon Account Health guide.

1. Confirm what Amazon actually deactivated

Review the notification in Seller Central, including Performance Notifications and Performance → Account Health. Determine whether the action applies to your entire seller account, a particular marketplace, one ASIN or a fulfilment privilege. Save the original notice, relevant dates and case references. An inactive listing is not the same as a deactivated selling account.

2. Identify the stated reason

Possible reasons include identity or business verification, linked-account concerns, authenticity or intellectual-property complaints, restricted products, or order-performance and policy violations. The evidence required varies. Do not assume that every deactivation requires the same response or a traditional Plan of Action.

3. Assemble evidence that matches the notice

  • Business verification: confirm legal entity, address and identity documents match the details requested.
  • Product authenticity: organize legitimate supplier invoices and traceability records for the products and dates identified.
  • Linked accounts: investigate the relationship and document the facts rather than opening another account to avoid enforcement.
  • Operational violations: reconcile affected orders, inventory, tracking and customer-service records to identify the root cause.

Never alter invoices, fabricate evidence or provide information you cannot substantiate.

4. Submit through the correct Seller Central workflow

Start in Performance → Account Health and use the reactivation or appeal workflow shown for your account. Amazon may ask for documents, verification steps, a questionnaire, an acknowledgement or a written explanation. Follow the account-specific instructions and review any requests for additional information before resubmitting. Amazon's published account-deactivation appeal guidance provides background, but the notice in your account takes priority.

5. If Amazon requests a Plan of Action, make it specific

Explain the documented root cause, what you corrected and the preventive controls you implemented. Connect each action to the stated violation. For example, if an inventory-sync failure caused orders for unavailable stock, describe the correction, order reconciliation and monitoring changes. Generic promises to “be more careful” do not establish what changed. If the notice is factually incorrect, provide evidence supporting that position rather than admitting an unverified violation.

6. What to do if an appeal is rejected

Read the response and identify the exact gap: missing documents, inconsistent account details, an unresolved policy issue or an explanation that does not address the cause. Correct that gap through the submission route Amazon specifies. Avoid repeated identical appeals or multiple unnecessary cases. There is no universal reinstatement timeline, and no consultant can guarantee an outcome.

7. Canada-specific checks

Verify the affected marketplace and registered business entity, especially if you sell on both Amazon.ca and Amazon.com or use a trade name. Make sure documentation matches the entity Amazon is asking to verify. Do not assume that advice for another marketplace or violation applies to your case.

8. When professional help makes sense

Consider support when the notice is unclear, evidence is spread across teams or suppliers, or a previous appeal was rejected. A structured review can help you identify the issue, organize documentation and prepare an accurate response. Frequently asked questions

Can a deactivated Amazon seller account be reinstated?

Some accounts can be reinstated after the required review and corrective steps, but the outcome depends on the specific issue and Amazon's decision.

Does every Amazon deactivation need a Plan of Action?

No. Some cases require verification documents or other steps. Follow the workflow shown in Seller Central.

Where do I appeal an Amazon account deactivation?

Review Performance → Account Health in Seller Central and follow the available reactivation or appeal instructions for your account.

What if Amazon rejects my appeal?

Review the rejection for missing or inadequate evidence, address the specific deficiency and respond through the designated workflow.

This guide provides general information, not a guarantee of reinstatement. Amazon policies and Seller Central processes can change. Simple Consulting is independent and is not affiliated with Amazon.
