Short answer: Walmart Canada charges two core WFS fees: a per-unit fulfilment fee based on shipping weight, and a monthly storage fee based on cubic feet. In Walmart Canada's fee guide (last updated October 28, 2026), fulfilment starts at $5.50 per unit for items under 1 lb and standard storage is $0.80 per cubic foot per month. There are no signup or monthly subscription fees. Whether WFS works for a product comes down to its packaged dimensions, how fast it sells and how long it sits in storage.

Fees and promotions change. Use the figures below as a working reference, then confirm them in Walmart's current Canadian documentation and fee calculator before you commit inventory.

1. Fulfilment fees by shipping weight

The fulfilment fee covers picking, packing and shipping each unit to the customer. Walmart Canada publishes these per-unit rates:

WFS Canada fulfilment fees (CAD, per unit)
Shipping weightFee per unit
Under 1 lb$5.50
1 to 3.9 lb$6.50
4 to 6.9 lb$8.50
7 to 11 lb$10.10

Heavier units follow per-pound steps in Walmart's table, with listed rates such as $18.00 at 21 lb and $24.00 at 31 lb and additional per-pound charges between tiers, up to 500 lb. For anything over 11 lb, read the current table rather than extrapolating from these rows.

Two other charges can apply: an oversize fee (section 3) and a fuel surcharge of up to 7% of the fulfilment fee when fuel prices rise above $2 per litre.

2. How Walmart calculates shipping weight

This is where most estimates go wrong. Walmart does not simply use the weight on your spec sheet:

  1. For units under 1 lb or over 150 lb, it uses the unit weight.
  2. For everything else, it uses the greater of the unit weight or the volumetric weight. Volumetric weight is length × width × height in inches, divided by 300.
  3. It adds 0.25 lb for packaging, then rounds up to the next whole pound.

A light but bulky product can therefore be charged as a much heavier one. Measure the packaged unit, not the product inside it.

3. Oversize fees

For units up to 150 lb, Walmart adds an oversize fee on top of the fulfilment fee:

WFS Canada oversize fees (CAD, per unit)
ConditionFee
Longest side over 48 in, median side over 30 in, or longest side plus girth over 105 in$4.00
Longest side over 87 in, or longest side plus girth over 118 in$35.00

Girth is 2 × (median side + shortest side). A box that is 49 inches long pays the $4.00 surcharge even if it is light.

4. Storage fees

Storage is charged per cubic foot per month, based on a 30-day month. Cubic feet are length × width × height in inches, divided by 1,728.

WFS Canada storage fees (per cubic foot per month)
SituationRate
Off-peak months, stored under 365 days$0.80
Peak months (usually October to December), stored up to 30 days$0.80
Peak months, stored more than 30 days$0.80 plus an additional $1.70
Stored more than 12 months$7.00

The long-term rate is the one to watch. At $7.00 per cubic foot, a slow-moving bulky item can cost more to store for a month than it earns on a sale. Send quantities you expect to sell, and review aged stock well before it crosses the 12-month mark.

6. Current promotions

Walmart Canada is running WFS discounts until January 31, 2027. Sellers who went live with WFS on or after February 1, 2026 get 25% off fulfilment fees and 50% off off-peak storage, which would bring the small product above from $6.50 to about $4.88 per unit. Existing sellers can get up to 20% off fulfilment and up to 60% off off-peak storage, depending on how much of their marketplace sales go through WFS and how many days of inventory they hold. Walmart's Preferred Carrier fee and its removal and disposal fee are also discounted 100% during the same period.

Discounts do not stack, and Walmart can change or end them at any time. Model each SKU at full price first. If it only works at a promotional rate, decide now what you will do when the promotion ends.

7. What the WFS fee does not include

Fulfilment and storage are not the whole cost of selling through WFS. Your model also needs:

  • Walmart's referral fee on each sale, which varies by category
  • Inbound freight to the fulfilment centre
  • Labelling and prep, through your own team or Walmart's prep service
  • Returns, including units that come back unsellable
  • Advertising, if you run Walmart Connect campaigns

Compare that total with what the same order costs today through your own warehouse or 3PL. 8. How to keep WFS fees under control

  • Check packaging before you list. Taking an inch off a carton can move a product into a lower weight tier or under the 48-inch oversize threshold.
  • Send slow movers in smaller, more frequent shipments so units don't age into peak or long-term storage rates.
  • Review aged inventory monthly. Decide whether to promote, remove or stop replenishing a SKU before it reaches 12 months.
  • Check the dimensions on file. If Walmart's recorded package dimensions are larger than the real package, each order can be charged at the wrong tier. Compare fees on your settlement report with what your measurements say they should be.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Walmart WFS cost in Canada?

WFS has two core fees: a fulfilment fee per unit, starting at $5.50 for items under 1 lb, and a storage fee of $0.80 per cubic foot per month in standard months. There are no signup or subscription fees. Your total cost also includes Walmart's referral fee, inbound freight, prep and returns.

Does Walmart WFS charge by dimensional weight?

Yes. For units between 1 lb and 150 lb, Walmart uses the greater of the actual weight or the volumetric weight (length × width × height in inches divided by 300), adds 0.25 lb for packaging and rounds up to the next pound.

When does Walmart charge WFS peak storage fees?

Peak months are usually October to December. Units stored for more than 30 days during peak months are charged an additional $1.70 per cubic foot per month on top of the standard $0.80.

What is the WFS long-term storage fee in Canada?

Items stored for more than 12 months are charged $7.00 per cubic foot per month, so aged inventory should be reviewed well before it reaches that point.
