How to Set Up Remarketing Campaigns That Actually Convert

By Scott Nguyen · Jun 5, 2024 · 1 min read

Remarketing — showing ads to people who have previously visited your website — is one of the most efficient forms of digital advertising. But most remarketing campaigns are set up as a single audience seeing a single ad. The opportunity is in the segmentation.

Segment by Behavior, Not Just Visit

A visitor who spent 30 seconds on your homepage is fundamentally different from someone who added a product to cart and didn't check out. They should see different ads with different messages. The homepage visitor needs awareness messaging. The cart abandoner needs urgency and a direct offer.

The Most Valuable Remarketing Audiences

Cart abandoners are your highest-intent remarketing audience — they were moments away from buying. Product page viewers are next — they showed interest in specific products. Blog readers are brand-aware but less ready to buy. Your past customers are the most valuable remarketing audience for cross-sell and upsell campaigns.

Frequency Caps Prevent Ad Fatigue

Seeing the same ad too many times damages brand perception and wastes budget on impressions that are generating no additional lift. Cap your remarketing frequency at 3–5 impressions per user per day. Beyond that, you're paying to annoy people.

Time Your Remarketing to the Purchase Cycle

If your product has a 7-day purchase cycle (the time from first consideration to purchase), run your remarketing audience at a 14-day window. If the cycle is 30 days, use a 45-day window. Remarketing outside the purchase cycle reaches people who have already made their decision and wastes your budget.

The fix: Create a separate remarketing campaign for cart abandoners with a message specifically addressing why someone might abandon — price, trust concerns, needing more information. This single segmentation typically delivers 2–3x the ROAS of a generic remarketing campaign.
