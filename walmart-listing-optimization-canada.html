Walmart listing optimization · Canada

Walmart listings built for accurate catalogue data and a clearer customer offer.

I help brands improve Walmart Marketplace item setup and customer-facing content, then keep the catalogue work connected to publishing status, inventory and the account tasks required to maintain it.

Start with item setup and required data

Before rewriting content, I review the item record, category, identifiers, required attributes and variation structure for the marketplace you use. Canadian and US requirements are checked separately.

  • Titles and product descriptions
  • Images and available rich content
  • Attributes, identifiers and variants
  • Publishing status and item-level account messages

Improve the customer-facing listing

The goal is to make the product easy to identify and understand using accurate product information. Content work is based on the actual item, customer questions and the fields available in the marketplace rather than copying another retailer.

Work through unpublished and catalogue issues

If an item is not publishing correctly, I review the stated errors, item data and account notices to identify the next action. Some problems require data changes; others require evidence or marketplace support follow-up.

Maintain the catalogue after launch

Listing optimization is not a one-time rewrite. New variants, inventory changes, content updates and platform requirements create recurring work. Ongoing support can keep those tasks in a visible queue with ownership and follow-up.

Questions before we start

Can you help with unpublished Walmart items?

Yes. I review item status, feed or setup errors and account notices to identify what is blocking publication. Walmart determines the final publication outcome.

Do you work with Walmart Canada and Walmart US?

Yes, where the engagement includes those marketplaces. Country-specific data, services and requirements are treated separately.

Can you optimize an existing Walmart catalogue?

Yes. We can start with a priority assortment, identify recurring data or presentation issues and work through the highest-impact items first.

Does listing optimization include Walmart advertising?

Advertising is a separate but connected workstream. If you need campaign management as well, see Walmart Connect management.

What keeps landing back on your desk?

Tell me about the channel or recurring issue. We will talk through where help would make a difference and a practical next step.
