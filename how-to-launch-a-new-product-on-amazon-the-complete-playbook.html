How to Launch a New Product on Amazon: The Complete Playbook

By Scott Nguyen · Published Jan 22, 2025 · Updated 2026-09-15 · 1 min read

In this article
  1. Pre-Launch: Build the Foundation
  2. Day 1–14: Drive Velocity
  3. Day 14–30: Build Reviews
  4. Day 30–90: Optimize and Scale

A product launch needs a ready listing, available inventory and a plan for reaching relevant shoppers. Define the work, budget and measures before the launch date.

Pre-Launch: Build the Foundation

Before stock becomes available, check product information, images, required attributes, identifiers and eligible enhanced content. Confirm the listing and fulfilment setup are ready.

Day 1–14: Drive Velocity

If you plan introductory advertising or promotions, define a budget and acceptable economics. Early sales can provide evidence, but they do not guarantee a future ranking position.

Day 14–30: Build Reviews

Enrol in Vine for your first reviews if eligible. Use the Request a Review button on every order. Early reviews establish credibility and improve conversion rate, which in turn improves your organic ranking.

Day 30–90: Optimize and Scale

Once you have baseline data — conversion rate, click-through rate, which keywords are driving sales — start optimizing. Shift PPC budget toward the highest-converting search terms. Adjust pricing based on Buy Box performance. Refine your listing based on customer questions and reviews.

The fix: Build a launch calendar with specific milestones for listing optimization, inventory arrival, PPC launch, and review generation. Treat it as a project, not a listing.
