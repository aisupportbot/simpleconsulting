Google Ads management focused on qualified enquiries and profitable sales.

Campaign and search-term review

Check whether your targeting and campaign structure match what you sell and whom you want to reach. Budget decisions should consider relevance, conversion quality, available evidence and the time it takes a customer to decide.

  • Search campaign structure and ad relevance
  • Search-term review and appropriate exclusions
  • Budget and bidding settings against business goals
  • Landing-page relevance and enquiry paths

Shopping feeds and product campaigns

Review product data and campaign decisions together. Incomplete feeds, unavailable inventory or unclear product pages can limit what advertising can achieve.

  • Merchant Center product data and reported issues
  • Product selection and feed attributes
  • Shopping and Performance Max review where relevant
  • Margin and stock considerations before scaling spend

Measure enquiries and sales correctly

A button click is different from a submitted enquiry or a completed booking. We review what the account counts as a conversion and how those records compare with actual sales or qualified leads.

Questions before we start

How do you judge campaign performance?

We use measures suited to the business: qualified enquiries, sales, contribution and the cost of acquiring them. Reporting compares the agreed baseline with the results while accounting for demand and measurement limits.

Can you review my current agency's work?

Yes. A second review can focus on a specific question. We define access and scope and share practical findings for your team to consider.

Is Google Ads the same service as Amazon PPC?

No. They use different accounts, targeting and reporting. Google Ads work has its own scope; Amazon advertising is covered on the Amazon management page.
