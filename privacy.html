Privacy & enquiries

About the information you share here.

Use the contact form, email or booking link to start a conversation with Scott at Simple Consulting.

Enquiry information

The contact form asks for your name, email and message. You can optionally choose a service. It also includes the page or service source to help explain your enquiry. Formspree processes the form and delivers it to Scott. The information is used to respond to your request and discuss the work you are considering.

Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing emails. Please do not include passwords, payment-card information or sensitive customer records in this form. We can agree on a suitable way to share relevant project records if needed.

Booking a call

The booking link opens Calendly. You can also choose to load its calendar on this page. Information you provide there is processed by Calendly for booking. Details typed into the message form are not automatically forwarded to the calendar.

The optional assessment

The assessment creates a summary from the answers you select. Its answers remain in your browser tab until you choose to share them. Adding the summary to the message form does not send it; review the message and press Send when ready.

Third-party services

Formspree, Calendly, the website host and your email provider may process information as part of delivering their services. Their policies explain their handling of that information. This website does not automatically load an advertising or analytics tracker in the current version. Recognised campaign labels from a link, such as Google or LinkedIn and the named service, may be retained in this browser tab for up to 30 minutes and included with an enquiry you submit. This helps Scott understand which campaign led to the conversation. Arbitrary link parameters, advertising click IDs and your typed form details are not stored for this purpose.

Formspree privacy policy · Calendly privacy policy · GitHub privacy statement

Questions about your information

Email scott@simpleconsulting.ca to ask about your enquiry or request correction or deletion. Scott will respond to discuss the request and any relevant record-keeping requirements.
