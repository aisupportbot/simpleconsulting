Before you send it

Questions people ask first

Do you need access to my Seller Central account?

No account access is needed for the assessment or first conversation. If later work needs access, use the marketplace’s own user permissions and grant only the access needed. Never share your password.

What does it cost?

The first 30-minute channel review is free. Any further project or ongoing work is scoped and priced with you before it begins.

Am I too small for this to be worth it?

The right scope depends on your stage, channels and needs. A focused project or a first conversation may be more suitable than ongoing management for an early-stage business.

I already have an agency. Is this a waste of time?

You can discuss a specific issue or a second review without changing your existing agency arrangement. We would agree on a clear scope and avoid duplicating work.

Will I get put on a mailing list?

Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing emails. The form uses Formspree to deliver your message to Scott.

Are you actually available?

I work with a small number of brands at a time, so occasionally I'm full. If I am, I'll tell you in the first reply and give you a realistic date rather than stringing it out.
