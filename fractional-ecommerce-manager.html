Fractional e-commerce support

An experienced e-commerce lead, for the work you need owned.

When you are the person joining up every channel, report and provider, the business needs more of your time than it should. I provide fractional e-commerce management to coordinate priorities and take on agreed operating responsibilities.

Book a free callSend a message

30 minutes · Work directly with Scott · No preparation needed

Connect the commercial and operating decisions

Listings, advertising, buying and fulfilment affect each other. I help bring those decisions into one plan so priorities are clear and teams or providers know what they own.

  • Channel priorities and launch planning
  • Coordination with marketing, finance and operations
  • Inventory and advertising decisions considered together
  • A visible record of tasks, owners and next steps

A clear owner for agreed responsibilities

I work alongside your team and providers to keep priorities moving. The engagement can combine channel oversight, coordination and hands-on tasks, with availability and decision responsibilities agreed at the start.

  • Marketplace account oversight and agreed execution
  • Internal-team and service-provider coordination
  • Regular performance and exception reviews
  • Documentation and handover as your team develops

Start with the work that needs attention

We identify the immediate issues, what information is missing and who owns the next actions. The first phase gives you a practical working plan and a regular way to review progress.

Project, ongoing management or fractional support?

A project suits a defined task. Ongoing account management suits recurring channel operations. Fractional support suits broader coordination and decisions across the business. We can discuss which arrangement matches your needs.

A good fit

For founders and teams coordinating several channels without a dedicated senior e-commerce lead.

What you can expect

A visible channel plan, clear task ownership and regular follow-through with your team and providers.

Questions before we start

Is this the same as hiring a full-time employee?

No. It is a scoped consulting engagement with agreed availability and responsibilities. It can provide support while your needs develop or while your internal team owns day-to-day tasks.

Will I work directly with Scott?

Yes. You work directly with me, including when coordinating tasks with your staff or existing providers.

Can you work alongside an agency?

Yes. We define ownership and approvals so the engagement addresses a gap and avoids duplicate effort.

Do you publish fixed retainer prices?

Fees are agreed after clarifying the channels, workload and responsibilities. The initial 30-minute conversation is free.
