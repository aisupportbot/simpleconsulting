Questions before we start

Do you support Amazon.ca and Amazon.com?

Yes. I support both storefronts. We review fulfilment, product requirements, catalogue and commercial decisions for each country rather than assuming one setup works everywhere.

Can you work alongside my internal team or agency?

Yes. We agree on which tasks I own, who approves changes and how reporting is shared. The scope can cover a specific operational gap.

Can you help with a restricted listing or account issue?

I can review the notice, relevant records and the required response, then help organise the next steps. Amazon makes listing and account decisions; the work focuses on the actual issue and applicable requirements.

What does account management cost?

The first 30-minute review is free. Project and ongoing fees depend on the catalogue, account model and responsibilities, and are agreed before paid work begins.

What is the difference between an Amazon consultant and an account management agency?

A consultant may work directly with your team on a focused problem or an agreed set of account responsibilities. An agency may provide a broader team or bundled services. Compare the actual person doing the work, scope, reporting, pricing and who approves account changes.
