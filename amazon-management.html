Seller Central and FBA management

Keep catalogue work connected to stock, pricing and fulfilment. I work through listing issues, inbound planning, receiving discrepancies and the recurring account tasks your team needs help managing.

  • Product titles, bullets, attributes and search terms
  • A+ Content, Brand Stores and eligible variation structures
  • Replenishment planning, aged inventory and FBA versus merchant fulfillment
  • Account notices, listing suppression and documentation follow-up

Amazon Vendor Central support

Selling wholesale to Amazon requires a different operating process from selling directly to shoppers. I help coordinate purchase orders, shipment records, deductions and reporting, including businesses that use both account models.

  • Purchase order confirmations and fulfillment coordination
  • Shortage and chargeback reconciliation against supporting records
  • Allowance and commercial-term review
  • Reporting across vendor and seller activity

Amazon PPC and listing optimisation

Advertising decisions should reflect product economics and inventory. I review Sponsored Products and other eligible campaign formats, search terms, budgets and attribution alongside listing quality and stock cover.

  • Campaign and search-term review
  • ACoS and TACoS in the context of margins
  • Product detail page improvements and relevant search language
  • A documented review cadence and agreed performance measures

Questions before we start

Do you support Amazon.ca and Amazon.com?

Yes. I support both storefronts. We review fulfilment, product requirements, catalogue and commercial decisions for each country rather than assuming one setup works everywhere.

Can you work alongside my internal team or agency?

Yes. We agree on which tasks I own, who approves changes and how reporting is shared. The scope can cover a specific operational gap.

Can you help with a restricted listing or account issue?

I can review the notice, relevant records and the required response, then help organise the next steps. Amazon makes listing and account decisions; the work focuses on the actual issue and applicable requirements.

What does account management cost?

The first 30-minute review is free. Project and ongoing fees depend on the catalogue, account model and responsibilities, and are agreed before paid work begins.
