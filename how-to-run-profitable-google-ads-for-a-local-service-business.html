Google Ads

How to Run Profitable Google Ads for a Local Service Business

By Scott Nguyen · Published Dec 15, 2025 · Updated 2026-09-15

In this article
  1. Check Local Services Ads Eligibility
  2. Tight Geographic Targeting
  3. Service-Specific Landing Pages

Google Search ads can reach people looking for a local service. Whether they are commercially useful depends on demand, competition, the offer, lead quality and the setup.

Check Local Services Ads Eligibility

Check whether Local Services Ads are available for the category and location. Where eligible, compare actual lead quality and total cost with other campaign options.

Tight Geographic Targeting

Set your targeting radius to your actual service area — not a broad region. Clicks from outside your service area are wasted budget.

Service-Specific Landing Pages

Sending all local search traffic to your homepage dilutes relevance and reduces conversion. Build a dedicated landing page for each service you advertise locally, with the location confirmed, the service described, and an easy way to call or book.

The next step: Confirm whether Local Services Ads fit your category and area. Compare eligibility, onboarding requirements, lead quality and cost before choosing the campaign mix.
