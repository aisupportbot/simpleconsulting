How to Expand From Amazon to Walmart Without Killing Your Margins

By Scott Nguyen · Published May 14, 2025 · Updated 2026-09-15 · 1 min read

In this article
  1. Model the product economics
  2. Choose a fulfilment approach
  3. Prepare the catalogue for Walmart
  4. Plan a controlled launch

Expanding from Amazon to Walmart is a channel decision as well as a catalogue task. Start with the country you plan to sell in, the product economics and the operating work the launch creates.

Model the product economics

For each pilot SKU, record selling price, product cost, referral fees, fulfilment costs, inbound freight, expected returns and advertising assumptions. Use current fees for Walmart Canada or the US as applicable.

Choose a fulfilment approach

Compare WFS with your own fulfilment or a 3PL. Use actual dimensions and weights, expected sales, storage duration and receiving requirements. Do not assume Canadian and US WFS programmes have identical fees, badges or rules.

Prepare the catalogue for Walmart

Check the required attributes, identifiers, images and item-setup rules for your category. Review publishing errors in Seller Center. Use pricing notices and the applicable policy to diagnose an issue, rather than assuming every unpublished item has the same cause.

Plan a controlled launch

Start with an assortment your team can support. Define who owns feeds, pricing, inbound shipments, advertising and returns. Review availability, conversion and contribution before adding more SKUs.
