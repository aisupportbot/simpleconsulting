How to Build a Brand on Amazon, Not Just a Listing

By Scott Nguyen · Published Aug 7, 2024 · Updated 2026-09-15

In this article
  1. Check Brand Registry eligibility
  2. Use the content tools that fit the product
  3. Use reporting to guide improvements
  4. Keep marketing within the available tools

Building a brand on Amazon means giving customers consistent, accurate product information and using the brand tools your account is eligible to access.

Check Brand Registry eligibility

Review the current trademark, ownership and account-role requirements before applying. Enrolment and access to particular features depend on Amazon’s requirements and your account.

Use the content tools that fit the product

Brand Stores and eligible A+ Content can help explain the range, product differences and use cases. Confirm current Basic and Premium A+ requirements instead of assuming access from one qualification.

Use reporting to guide improvements

Check the available Brand Analytics dashboards and define the question you want to answer. Search and purchase data can help identify where product information or assortment needs attention.

Keep marketing within the available tools

Do not assume that an older customer-engagement programme remains available or that it provides an exportable customer email list. Confirm the current tools and permissions in your account. Build direct marketing through separately consented relationships.
