How to Use Heatmaps to Improve Your Website Conversion Rate

By Scott Nguyen · Published Mar 16, 2026 · Updated 2026-09-15

In this article
  1. What Heatmaps Reveal
  2. The Most Common Insights
  3. Tools That Work

Analytics tools tell you what visitors do. Heatmaps show you how they do it — where they click, how far they scroll, where they spend attention, and where they abandon.

What Heatmaps Reveal

Click maps show where visitors are clicking — including where they click expecting a link but find none. Scroll maps show how far down the page most visitors actually reach. Session recordings show individual visitor journeys including hesitations, rage clicks, and abandonment patterns.

The Most Common Insights

Visitors clicking on elements expecting them to be links. Most visitors not scrolling far enough to see your CTA. Form fields causing abandonment because they're confusing or ask for too much. Visitors spending disproportionate time on sections suggesting confusion.

Tools That Work

Evaluate a heatmap or session-replay tool using its current capabilities, privacy controls, consent requirements and cost. Mask sensitive fields and confirm what is collected before enabling it.

The next step: If session analysis is appropriate for your site, configure the selected tool with suitable privacy settings and consent handling. Review a representative period and test one useful improvement from the evidence.
