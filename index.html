A few questions before we talk.

Can you help with just one issue?

Yes. We can start with a focused project, such as a listing problem, campaign review, inventory plan or records reconciliation. You do not need to begin with ongoing management.

Can you work with my existing team or agency?

Yes. I can own a defined part of the work and coordinate with the people already involved. We make the responsibilities clear at the start.

What happens on the first call?

We spend 30 minutes discussing your channels, the issue and what you have already tried. I will explain where I may be able to help and what I would look at next. The call is free; any detailed review or implementation is priced separately.

What if I am not ready to book?

Send a short message instead. Tell me what is happening and include your store or website link if useful. I reply within one business day.

What keeps landing back on your desk?

Tell me about the channel or recurring issue. We will talk through where help would make a difference and a practical next step.
