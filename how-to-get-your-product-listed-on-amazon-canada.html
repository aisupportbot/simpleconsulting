How to Get Your Product Listed on Amazon Canada

By Scott Nguyen · Published Sep 8, 2025 · Updated 2026-09-15 · 1 min read

In this article
  1. Confirm account and product eligibility
  2. Prepare the Canadian offer
  3. Compare fulfilment options
  4. Launch with operating ownership

Launching on Amazon Canada requires a country-specific review of your account, product information, fulfilment and economics. An existing US listing is a starting point, not proof that every requirement is satisfied.

Confirm account and product eligibility

Review the Canadian account setup and category requirements in Seller Central. Check identifiers, brand permissions and product documentation before listing.

Prepare the Canadian offer

Confirm pricing, currency, content, fees and available stock. Review applicable product and language requirements with the appropriate specialist where needed; these can depend on the product and where it is sold.

Compare fulfilment options

Model Canadian FBA, merchant fulfilment and any eligible cross-border programme. Compare delivery promises, fees, returns and inventory requirements. Do not assume a particular cross-border method removes or guarantees Prime eligibility.

Launch with operating ownership

Assign responsibility for catalogue updates, stock, advertising, account notices and reconciliations. Start with an assortment you can support and review the actual performance before expanding.
