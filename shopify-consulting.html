Shopify & DTC

A Shopify store that works with the rest of your business.

Help customers find the right product, understand the offer and complete their purchase. I improve store content and customer journeys while keeping inventory, fulfilment and marketplace decisions connected.

Store and product-page improvements

Help visitors understand what the product is, whether it fits their needs and what happens after they order. I review navigation, product information, purchase paths and the questions customers need answered.

  • Store structure, collections and product information
  • Mobile shopping and checkout-path review
  • Shipping, returns and support information

Connect Shopify with your wider business

A store improvement should work with your inventory and fulfilment setup. I review how product, order and stock information moves between your store, marketplaces, warehouse and business systems.

  • Catalogue and inventory coordination
  • Order and fulfilment handoffs
  • Channel pricing and promotional planning
  • Reporting requirements and recurring operating tasks

Support a launch or a focused project

The project might be a new storefront, a product-page refresh or a review of a specific customer journey. We agree on what is included, the platform limitations and how the result will be checked before implementation begins.

Measure the business outcome

Questions before we start

Can you help without rebuilding the whole store?

Yes. A focused review may identify navigation, product-content or fulfilment changes that can be made within your existing theme and setup.

Can you coordinate Shopify with Amazon and Walmart?

Yes. That is a useful fit for this service. The scope can connect store improvements with marketplace listings, inventory and fulfilment processes.

How do we decide what to improve first?

We review the purchase journey, available store data and recurring customer questions, then prioritise changes by likely business impact and the work involved. We agree on the baseline and measures for the project.

How do we get started?

Send your store URL and the issue you want help with, or book a free 30-minute review. Further work is scoped and priced before it begins.
