Marketplace reconciliation

Untangling returns, settlements and prior credits

Situation

Return and settlement records did not align cleanly, making it difficult to separate open discrepancies from payments or adjustments already issued.

Work

Normalized the review period, matched order and transaction identifiers, checked prior credits, isolated formula and quantity exceptions, and documented unresolved items for follow-up.

Deliverable

A cleaner reconciliation file with defined categories, supporting evidence and a next-action list. The marketplace remained responsible for eligibility and payment decisions.

How I work

Evidence first. Clear ownership. Follow-through.

I do not use a case study to imply that the same outcome is guaranteed for another account. The value is in the operating process: understand the records, define the issue, assign the work and keep it moving.

Start with the records

Use the actual marketplace reports, shipment records, catalogue data and operating context before deciding what the problem is.

Separate facts from assumptions

A discrepancy, error message or performance change is a reason to investigate—not automatically proof of a specific cause or recovery.

Make the next action visible

Every review should end with an owner, evidence, next step and way to confirm what happened.
