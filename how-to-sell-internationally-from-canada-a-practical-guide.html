How to Sell Internationally From Canada: A Practical Guide

By Scott Nguyen · Published Jan 12, 2026 · Updated 2026-09-15

In this article
  1. Start With the US
  2. Understand Regulatory Differences
  3. Logistics for Cross-Border Fulfillment

Canada's e-commerce market is constrained by population. The most significant growth opportunities lie in international expansion — primarily the US, UK, and Australia, where language, payment infrastructure, and regulatory frameworks are most similar.

Start With the US

The US market is 10x the size of Canada's, operates in USD (a currency advantage for Canadian businesses), and is accessible through existing channels like Amazon and Shopify with minimal additional setup.

Understand Regulatory Differences

Product, labelling and import requirements differ by destination and category. Identify the applicable regulator and requirements for the actual product before selling. A certification or approval in Canada does not by itself establish eligibility elsewhere.

Logistics for Cross-Border Fulfillment

Shipping from Canadian warehouses to US customers is expensive and slow relative to US domestic shipping. For significant US volume, establishing US-based fulfillment through Amazon FBA US or a US 3PL is essential for cost-competitiveness.

The fix: If you're a Canadian business selling primarily in Canada, calculate what your revenue would look like at your current conversion rate with US traffic. If the number is significant, US expansion warrants a structured evaluation.
