Retail marketplace & vendor onboarding · Canada

Retail-channel onboarding with the catalogue, fulfilment and operations connected.

I help Canadian brands prepare for and operate additional retail channels beyond Amazon and Walmart. The work can cover marketplace onboarding, vendor setup, product data, fulfilment, EDI or integration coordination, returns and recurring account operations.

Separate marketplace and wholesale/vendor models

A third-party marketplace account and a wholesale or first-party vendor relationship create different economics, data flows, service requirements and responsibilities. I help define the model before the team builds the operating process around it.

Prepare the product and business records

Organise item identifiers, product content, images, pricing or wholesale cost inputs, banking and insurance information, shipping details and other records required for the specific retailer relationship.

Coordinate fulfilment, EDI and system handoffs

Retail programmes can create new order, inventory, ASN, invoicing, return and routing requirements. I help translate those requirements into owners, systems and exception processes rather than treating integration as a checkbox.

Build a repeatable operating rhythm

After launch, keep purchase orders or marketplace orders, item issues, inventory, deductions, returns and account follow-up visible. The scope can be a focused onboarding or ongoing channel support.

Questions before we start

Which retailers can you support?

My experience includes Best Buy Canada, Costco, Wayfair, Home Depot and other retail channels. The exact scope depends on the account model and current retailer requirements.

Can you guarantee retailer approval or a purchase order?

No. The retailer controls seller, supplier and assortment decisions. I can help prepare the operating and onboarding work around the opportunity.

Can you help with EDI and integrations?

Yes. I can help define the business requirements, coordinate the implementation and verify the resulting order, inventory or document flow with the relevant systems or partners.

Can this be ongoing support after launch?

Yes. The engagement can continue into catalogue, orders, inventory, reporting, deductions, returns and other recurring retail-channel work.

What keeps landing back on your desk?

Tell me about the channel or recurring issue. We will talk through where help would make a difference and a practical next step.
