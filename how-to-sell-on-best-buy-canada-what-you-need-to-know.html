How to Sell on Best Buy Canada: What You Need to Know

By Scott Nguyen · Published Oct 23, 2024 · Updated 2026-09-15

In this article
  1. Marketplace and retail are different models
  2. Model the specific proposal
  3. Prepare content and operations
  4. Start with a launch checklist

Best Buy Canada has distinct Marketplace and retail-vendor relationships. Confirm which route you are pursuing before modelling the terms, preparing product data or arranging fulfilment.

Marketplace and retail are different models

A Marketplace seller offers products through BestBuy.ca under the marketplace arrangement. A retail-vendor relationship involves supplying Best Buy under agreed buying terms. The onboarding, payment, service and return responsibilities differ.

Model the specific proposal

Use the fees, wholesale cost or margin requirements, freight, advertising commitments and return terms applicable to your proposed agreement. Do not substitute a generic margin percentage for the actual offer.

Prepare content and operations

Confirm the product categories, identifiers, content, image and language requirements. Agree on order handling, inventory updates, delivery, returns and customer-service responsibilities. EDI or an integration should be assessed for the particular relationship.

Start with a launch checklist

Record the required documents, insurance or banking information, product data, fulfilment arrangements and open decisions. Obtain the current requirements directly from Best Buy before committing inventory.
