WFS questions

Are Canadian and US WFS requirements the same?

Do not assume they are. Programme availability, fees, reports and operating requirements should be checked for the country and account you use.

Can you help with WFS onboarding?

Yes. The work can include readiness, product selection, item setup, inbound planning and the operating responsibilities that need an owner. Walmart determines account and programme eligibility.

Can you compare WFS with Amazon FBA?

Yes. Compare the two at SKU level using current marketplace fees, fulfilment, storage, inbound freight, returns, advertising assumptions and the inventory commitment required.

Can you review WFS reimbursements?

Yes. I can reconcile receiving, return and settlement records and assess potential claim eligibility. Walmart determines whether a claim is approved.

Tell me about the channel or recurring issue. We will talk through where help would make a difference and a practical next step.
