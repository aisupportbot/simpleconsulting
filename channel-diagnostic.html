Questions

Do I need to share my password?

No. Use the marketplace’s permission controls to add a user with the access needed, or export the reports. Access is agreed before the review starts.

Can my own team carry out the plan?

Yes. The plan is written so your team, an agency or Scott can carry it out. Implementation is separate unless you choose ongoing support.

Not sure the diagnostic is the right first step?

Book the free 30-minute call first. If a focused project or a different starting point fits better, Scott will say so.

Are taxes included?

No. The price is CAD $750 plus applicable tax. Marketplace fees, advertising spend and third-party costs are not included.
