Most marketplace problems aren't strategy problems. They're operational ones. A suppressed listing nobody noticed. Reimbursements never claimed. Ad spend going to search terms that will never convert. A WFS fee model that quietly turned a profitable SKU unprofitable.

I've spent fifteen years finding and fixing exactly those things — across Amazon Vendor Central and Seller Central, FBA, Walmart Marketplace and WFS, plus Best Buy Canada, Wayfair, Costco and Home Depot. That means when I look at your account, I'm not learning the platform on your budget. I've filed the chargeback disputes, negotiated the co-op terms, planned the Q4 inbound shipments, and argued with support about a reimbursement claim. I know where the money hides.

I work with a small number of brands at a time, which means you deal with me directly — not an account coordinator two layers removed from the work.
